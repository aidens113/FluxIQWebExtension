# Observed control intent

The web domain classifies authoring controls once per retained sanitized capture with `collectWebLlmControlIntents`. It indexes actual retained handles and walks at most forty same-frame ancestors per element. The selected control or nearest acting ancestor supplies its own wording, bounded to eighty characters. Container prose and a close glyph inside an accepting button cannot substitute for that button's meaning. Known accepting wording wins over a contradictory nested control or an additional positive act following a decline.

The private result maps handles to `accept`, `dismiss`, `neutral` or `navigate`. A consent acceptance remains `accept`. Its optional `responseOnly: true` annotation requires a closed positive consent answer and actual membership in an observed consent dialog or cover, through sanitized modal membership or ancestry. An offer defeats the annotation. Coverage alone, saved dialog names, unknown layers, other frames and malformed ancestry supply no such annotation. A bare “Accept all” remains accepting without the annotation. Reject and necessary-only choices remain authoring preferences; the classifier does not enforce their availability.

Classification takes no snapshot and makes no provider or browser call. The result records a bounded interpretation of untrusted page evidence. It grants no permission, establishes no effect class or repeat safety, and does not prove that a page's control does what its label says. Automatic interference clearing has a separate, stricter vocabulary and remains unchanged. Intent is contextual retained evidence, never a saved fingerprint or a model-authored parameter.

A host draft retains the resolved selector and whole fingerprint of an actual act. The same control can then appear on several captured pages or documents, making a later absolute lookup match several sources. Unscoped completion or historical resolution may distinguish these views only through one exact original pre-act record already retained privately by this Flow. Its selector, whole identity, frame, shadow context and captured source must match; a second acted source, a shared address or any competing semantic conflict still refuses. Observing an identical control or supplying model-written source fields establishes no lineage. A live call scoped to its current page uses that page's captured document and cannot borrow an older pre-act annotation. No provenance or intent is added to the durable node, so saved Flow execution continues to reacquire its fingerprint after navigation or reload.
