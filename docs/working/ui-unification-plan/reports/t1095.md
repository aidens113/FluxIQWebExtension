## The single path (decision, recorded in the working doc)

A person opens the extension and types what they want. FluxIQ picks the person's current project, or makes their first
one, and builds the Flow there. Every chat belongs to a project and lives in that project's conversation store, so the
panel shows the same conversation; "Open in FluxIQ panel" lands on it (`/chats/<id>?project=<projectId>`). The person's
current project and open Flow are one per-person value in Core: the panel and every client write it on a switch and
follow it (the panel live, through a `context` event on its change stream). Keys, humanness and project creation or
management are in the panel; the extension links to them.
