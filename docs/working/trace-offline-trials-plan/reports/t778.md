## What changed and why

### 1. Extension: a save's subject is its item's title, not the Apply link beside it

**Cause, confirmed.** The pane heart is in no row, so `recordSubject` falls back to `nearestTitle`. `nearestTitle` stopped
at the actions bar, `[a "Apply on company site" target=_blank][span heart]`, and took the link's words as the bar's title.
Reverted, the headed spec shows all three saves declaring `s:3d32b2796f39691d27b63e2b4898af06`, the same token w11 found.

**Fix. Two generic rules, read from markup, never from words:**

- **New `identity/control-bar.ts` (`holdsOnlyControls`).** A part that holds nothing but controls is a bar of actions:
  - controls are links, buttons, fields, svg drawings, and control roles;
  - so a link in such a bar is one of the actions, not a title.

  `nearestTitle` skips that part, and it also skips a link inside the bar when a higher part offers it again. The walk
  then reaches the pane head's `h2`, the job title. A part that says more than its controls keeps its link as its title:
  for example, a saved line with an item link, a price, and Move to cart.
- **New `identity/action-link.ts` (`isActionLink`).** A link that acts on the page is never a title, in `readRowTitle`
  and therefore everywhere. That means:
  - an `href` that is `#…`, empty, or `javascript:`;
  - a `download` link;
  - a role other than `link`.

  This covers the brief's "Share/Save as a link" case.
- **Rejected:** treating `target=_blank` as an action link. Crossborder, local-classifieds, and everything-store sponsored
  cards all open their *title* links in a new tab.

**One existing expectation changed:** `record.test.ts`, "a detail page's lone add".

- The buy box `[a "Sold by Fernhill Goods"][button "Add to basket"]` is now a bar of controls.
- The add is about the page heading, KETTLE, where it used to be about "Sold by Fernhill Goods".
- KETTLE is the same words the basket line it creates is about, which matches the brief's rule.

The test is renamed and has a comment explaining this.

`record.ts` grew from 750 to 777 lines, which is under the 800 limit.

### 2. Domain: a lasting press or a toggle is never pressed again on the domain's own look

**Cause, confirmed headed.** `press-effect/settled.ts` (t513) pressed a lasting press a second time when the page view was
unchanged through the 1.5 s wait and the control was still shown and enabled. The second and third Unsaves on My jobs
met exactly that:

- the row stays until the page loads;
- the heart repaint is not in the view;
- the notice still read "Removed from saved jobs".

Reverted, the headed spec shows `pressedAgain: "Pressed again: the first press of "Unsave job" (t215) changed nothing…"`
on undo 2.

**Why the domain look cannot be the fresh read.** The extension's click (`content/actions/click.ts`) already watches every
press for a request, a navigation, a focus move, or a change in its section:

- With no sign at all, the extension presses again itself.
- A press that settles exactly as it was answers `web.action.no_effect` with `certainNoEffect`, and Core's retries make it
  once more (t517). This is social's Post.

So any press that reaches `settled.ts` as a success was answered by the page in a way the domain cannot see. That made
the t513 domain re-press blind.

**Fix:**

- **New `press-effect/repeat-press.ts` (`webPressMayRepeat`)** decides whether a press may be made again here:
  - a press whose call declared a lasting consequence: never;
  - a press on a control that holds a state a press flips (`aria-pressed`, `aria-checked`, `checked`, or `expanded`):
    never, whatever was declared;
  - anything else, such as Load more: still pressed once more after the wait, as in t511.
- **New `press-effect/uncertain.ts` (`webPressUncertain`)** gives the sentence for a press that is held back. It is not
  "no effect". It says the press was not pressed a second time because it could do the act twice or turn it back, and to
  read again where the effect shows (load the page again, or read the list or control) before pressing again.
- **`settled.ts`** returns `{ presses: 1, uncertain }` for those presses, after the wait's looks.
- **`run.ts` / `outcome.ts`** gain a new outcome field, `effectUncertain`:
  - the command's status stands (`succeeded`), where a held-back lasting press used to answer `no_effect`;
  - `unchangedPress` (the "press the same control once more" advice) is never given beside it.
  - `run.ts` stays at exactly 800 lines; the comment was trimmed to stay at the limit.
- **Removed `press-effect/certain-no-effect.ts`** (`webPressCertainlyIgnored`) and its barrel export. Nothing else used
  it, and the policy it encoded is retired.

**Tests whose expectations changed with the policy:**

- **`press-settled.test.ts`**, t513 section rewritten:
  - an undo landed while the list still draws the row: read again, never pressed again;
  - a lasting press in an open dialog: not pressed again here;
  - a lasting press whose effect shows during the wait: plain press;
  - toggles (pressed false/true, checkbox, disclosure, each lasting or not): read, never flipped back;
  - a Load more the page ignored: pressed once more.
- **`no-effect-press.test.ts`**:
  - the t513 "pressed once more" case became the real Post shape: the first dispatch answers `web.action.no_effect`
    `certainNoEffect`, and the retries press again and succeed (2 clicks, `attempts: 2`, no `pressedAgain`);
  - the lasting unchanged and lasting disabled cases are now one click with `effectUncertain`;
  - a new Bold toggle case is one click with `effectUncertain`.
- **`llm-evidence/tests/tools.test.ts`**, "reports a successful press with unchanged parsed evidence as no progress": its
  control is a disclosure (`aria-expanded`), so it is now pressed once with `effectUncertain` and status `succeeded`,
  where it used to be pressed twice with `no_effect`.

## Open questions or contradictions found

1. **The domain never reloads by itself.** "Read first" is carried by the `effectUncertain` words, and by Core's own load
   reads (t751 `lookFirst`). If the supervisor wants the domain to reload the page as the fresh read, that is a further
   change.
2. **Policy reach beyond undos.** The toggle rule also stops the t511 second press for non-lasting disclosures, checkboxes
   and `aria-pressed` controls. The extension still presses those once more when the page showed no sign at all.
3. **Held-back lasting presses now answer `status: succeeded` plus `effectUncertain`, where they used to answer
   `no_effect`.** Nothing in Core or downstream reads `"no_effect"` (grep), but the supervisor should know the status
   changed.
4. **`sanitize.ts` `pageSubject` still reads only an `h1`** (w11's optional point). It was not changed, because the pane
   save now carries its own subject.
5. **The bar rule treats only real controls as controls.** Those are links, buttons, fields, svg, and control roles. A
   bare `span tabindex` with words counts as the part's words, so a part like `[a item][span "Move to cart"]` keeps its
   link as its title. That is right there, but it is a heuristic edge.
