## Generic capabilities a realistic spec would need to prove instead

None of these is specific to its synthetic fixture. Each suggested realistic home comes from a quick search of the scenario sources. I have not confirmed any of them hosts a workflow that would exercise the capability.

1. **Extraction over a virtualised list, which silently returns only the render window** (admin-console, marked as a product defect). No realistic site has a virtualised list: the only "virtual" match, in bigbox-retail, is `shell/support-chat.ts`. No home exists today. This needs a realistic site with a long virtualised list, for example search results on professional-network or everything-store.
2. **A wheel scroll aimed at a scrollable inner pane** (admin-console, defect). Candidates: social-network-feed (`client/feed-script.ts`), and local-classifieds or photo-social feeds.
3. **Resolving a target inside an open shadow root** (admin-console, defect). Candidates: `attachShadow` appears in eight of the ten realistic sites, including everything-store, job-board, crossborder-marketplace and company-website.
4. **Same-document routing (pushState) and history** (admin-console). Candidates: local-classifieds (`client/feed-script.ts`), photo-social (`client/grid-script.ts`), professional-network (`search/people-client.ts`).
5. **An element replaced during an inline edit** (admin-console). No obvious realistic home found.
6. **Recovering identity after drift: a renamed, moved, or re-labelled control, and refusing a look-alike** (identity-drift). This needs the restyle or drift variants on the realistic sites, wherever their manifests declare them.
7. **Infinite scroll and load-more extraction, and a feed that ends early** (infinite-feed). Candidates: social-network-feed and photo-social (load-more); `IntersectionObserver` appears in six realistic sites.
8. **File download and upload steps** (file-transfer). Candidates: upload on job-board (`ats/embed-pages.ts`) and everything-store; download possibly in professional-network or social-network-feed (I did not confirm these).
9. **Sign-in, and stopping with `auth_required` when the session expires** (auth-gate). Candidates: sign-in on bigbox-retail and job-board. I found no expiry variant there.
10. **Header-mapped table extraction under column reorder, and sort persistence** (data-table). Candidates: sort controls on most realistic sites. A true table with column headers is unconfirmed.
