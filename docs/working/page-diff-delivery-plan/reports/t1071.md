## What changed and why

### F3: the exact refusing line, and the fix

**The refusing line is proven.** A reproduction drives the node runner through attempt 1 and attempt 2:
- look, add, look, click the link;
- then a new document, look, click the link by hand.

It gave exactly 0062's refusal, `web.handle.ambiguous` and `web.handle.ambiguous:parameters`. Temporary
instrumentation (since removed) showed where it comes from. It is `absolute.ts:73-74` (now `:74-75`), the
`agreed === undefined` branch, with `matches.size = 2` and `witnesses.size = 0`:
- the search page and the cart page both matched, each with intent `neutral`;
- there was no witness, because `acted` holds only attempt 1's press of "🛒 1 $3.97" (`target-packets.ts:396`).

**The fix is `control-intents/witnesses.ts` (new).** Every act is now kept as a witness of its own source: its
location, document (`timeOrigin`), frame and layer. Before, only the first act per handle was kept.
- **A later attempt's act is now a witness.** It is a new document, so it is a new source.
- **Two copies are kept** when they differ:
  - the control as the act's capture showed it;
  - the identity the act actually ran with, which is the views' agreement and what the draft keeps.
- **The first act per source still stands.** An undo in the same document never replaces it (t743).
- **What is unchanged:**
  - `acted`, still the first act per handle, still feeds the candidate identity restore (t442 F1);
  - `absolute.ts`'s rules are unchanged: zero witnesses, or any disagreement, still refuses.

**The carried step in attempts 3 and 4 is not reproduced.** With attempt 1's words it assembled as `unchanged` in my
simulation, not refused. See "Not verified".

### F1: words seen changing are a value, and the destination confirms

**Domain: `plan-resolution/self-changing/state-words.ts` (new), wired into `target-packets.ts`.**
- **What it keeps.** Per control, every wording its captures showed and the wordings its own act left in the same
  document. A control here is its address plus its tag, id, test id and label. It also keeps every destination
  path the control led to.
- **When words are state.** There must be at least two wordings that are not the control's own act's effect, and
  the link must always have led to one path.
- **How the step is marked.** The resolution's identity keeps the words and is marked `readsWords: true`. It also
  carries `href` set to that path. This applies:
  - to handle resolutions (`resolve`, `resolveOn`);
  - to a host absolute target at assembly (`absoluteIntent` returns marked `parameters`; `resolve-plan-node.ts`
    returns them as resolved).
- **The saved step in this run.** Attempt 1's step 10 is saved as "🛒 1 $3.97", `readsWords`,
  `href: /scenarios/bigbox-retail/cart`.
- **Why only a destination counts as what stays.** `resolve-plan-node.test.ts` showed that a test id is often the
  template's (`data-testid="product-link"` on every card). So an id, test id or label never qualifies a control.
  A selector a capture gave to several elements counts for none.
- **A control's own act is not state.** "Get coupons" to "Collected", or "Follow" to "Following", keep their words
  as identity (t442), in this attempt and later ones.
- **`absolute.ts` ignores the mark when comparing identity.** It leaves out `href`, as it already left out
  `readsWords`.

**Extension: `apps/extension/src/content/identity/destination/` (new), `veto.ts`, `score.ts`.**
- **The destination verdict.** A candidate is one of:
  - `confirms`: it is a link that is shown, is not an action link, stays on the same origin, has the recorded path,
    and no other visible link in its root leads there;
  - `shared`: another visible link leads there too;
  - `elsewhere`: anything else.
- **The veto.** For a target whose words are a value and that carries a root-relative `href`:
  - the destination counts as the question asked;
  - rule 2 accepts only `confirms`;
  - rule 1 (the score must be 0 or more) still weighs everything else.
- **Level 2.** The marked target is settled by the one confirming candidate among the leaders. If none confirms, it
  is `unmatched`. If a link is `shared`, it is a tie (`ambiguous`). Old words never settle it.
- **The `href` must be a root-relative path.** Recorded reads carry whole addresses, so they are not affected.

## Open questions or contradictions found

1. **The brief places `absolute.ts` in Core.** It is the domain's (`plan-resolution/control-intents/absolute.ts`).
   Core was not touched.
2. **The mark reuses `readsWords`.** It carries the same meaning: the words are a value an earlier step may have
   changed, they never veto, and they break ties only for an unmarked read. It travels end to end today: wire
   (`output-nodes/targets.ts`), gateway, target-reacquire and extension. But the name says "reads", and a press
   now carries it.
   - Side effect: Core's trial feedback names such a step by nothing rather than by its old words
     (`candidate-trial/step-control.ts`).
   - A rename to something like `wordsAreValue` would touch the wire contract outside this brief's ownership.
3. **The destination rides in the fingerprint's existing `href` field, written as a root-relative path.** The
   extension treats only a `/`-path `href` beside `readsWords` as a kept destination, so recorded reads with a full
   address are untouched. That is a convention. A dedicated field would need a wire-contract change.
4. **When the mini cart is open**, two visible links lead to the cart, so the step is refused as `ambiguous`. The
   destination cannot then tell the two links apart. This is by design ("unique on the page"), and the run's page
   had the mini cart closed.
