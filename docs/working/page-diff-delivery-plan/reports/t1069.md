# t1069 report

## Outcome
Done. A decision's edits are now atomic per step, not per decision.

## What changed and why
Core `packages/fluxiq/src/runtime/flow-authoring/draft/amendment/apply.ts`:
- Removed t1068's whole-decision rollback wrapper. `applyEach` now snapshots each step as found, tallies the edits applied per step, and records which step each amendment's blocking refusal (anything outside `NOT_BLOCKING`) struck.
- After the amendment loop, every struck step is restored to its found state and its applied edits subtracted, so 0144's `keep` and `result` on step 36 stand or fall together. The step's entries in the withdrawn, written and routed lists are dropped, and an act claimed onto it is handed back to the step it came from. Edits on other steps apply.
- The guards (strand check, repeat holds, span routing, settled repeats, loop-never-ends) are as before t1068: their put-back is told and the rest applies. One exception, to honour "same step stays all-or-none": if a repeat/hold/span guard takes back a repeat on a step that the decision also edited in another way (2+ edits), that step is restored as found (`repeat-hold.test.ts` shared-source test). The strand check is not subject to this.
- Every refusal still names its edit and reason. `no_such_step` names no step, so it blocks nothing else.

Tests (same folder `amendment/tests/` unless noted):
- Restored partial application (edits on different steps): `reach.test.ts` (drop of step 5 applies, 3 and 4 put back), `repeat-hold.test.ts` outer-repeat (both orders: the valid repeat stands), `drop-reversal.test.ts` (keep of step 5 stands beside the put-back drop), `../tests/look-positions.test.ts` (the act on step 3 lands beside the refused look).
- Kept all-or-none (same step): `repeat-hold.test.ts` shared-source extension, `result-atomic.test.ts` 0144 test. Its "good edit beside refused one" test now expects the other step's drop to apply.
- New `per-step.test.ts`: dropped-act's shape (stranding drop put back, other drops apply); plus a no-such-step beside edits test (weak: only asserts the refusal and applied >= 1).
- `answer-first/tests/dropped-act.test.ts` untouched, its expectation was right.

## Update: a struck edit leaves no trace (lead's follow-up)
Chosen route: strike before settling, by re-running. `applyAutomationStudioFlowDraftAmendments` now applies the decision, collects the steps a blocking refusal struck (plus steps a repeat/hold/span guard took a repeat from after two or more edits), restores EVERY step to its found state, and applies the decision again with the struck steps' amendments skipped (repeating until no new step is struck). Openers, reversals, act claims and the strand/hold/span/loop-covered settling therefore only ever see the final state. The struck edits' refusals are told first, once per struck step. The earlier in-place restore code is removed.
