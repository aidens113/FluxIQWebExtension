# t1065: A continuing judge always answers in a form Core reads (audit F1, F3, F7)

Core worktree `fxwork/t1065/!FluxIQ`, branch `task/t1065-judge-continuation-no-tools`. Paths below are under
`packages/fluxiq/src/runtime/`. Nothing is committed.

## Outcome

Done. The lead widened the brief mid-task to the whole of audit finding 1, plus F3 and F7, and recommended an answer
tool. This is what was built:

- **F1, answer tool.** Every Anthropic decider request sent as tools now ends with a judge answer tool,
  `core_check_answer`. Its input is the Flow-less judge's reply schema. A judge that continues the decider calls it,
  with `tool_choice` left at `auto` and the tool list byte for byte the decider's. The answer is read from that call
  alone. A decider that calls it is refused by name.
- **F1, answered in the conversation.** A continuing judge's reply that arrives and is not read is answered in that
  same conversation, and the verdict is asked for again there. That covers prose with no call, a call of a decision
  tool, and an answer that does not fit. It never goes to a judge that sees less.
- **F3, the decider's whole message.** A continuation now carries the decider's whole user turn, including the
  `core.current_view` tail after the last cache mark. The cached bytes are unchanged.
- **F7, stale wording.** The stale "leaving out looks" in the decision guidance is reworded.

My first pass read a verdict out of the prose at the end of a reply, and answered only a stray tool call. Both are
replaced: the audit rules out a lenient parse, and the lead asked for one channel.

## What changed and why

- **`models/providers/anthropic/judge-answer-tool.ts` (new).** The tool's name and description. The header records
  why it is a tool and why it is sent non-strict (see Open questions).
- **`llm/anthropic/request.ts`.**
  - Appends the answer tool, last, to every decider request sent as tools.
  - Adds `automationStudioAnthropicJudgeAnswerSchema`, the Flow-less judge's outputSchema. It depends only on the task
    kind, so the tool is the same bytes in every decision.
- **`models/providers/anthropic/continuation.ts`.**
  - F3: the prefix is the decider's whole user turn, and `callIds` resolve view references against that tail. The
    cache marks stay where the decider put them.
  - `answerTool` is set when the decider offered the answer tool.
  - `JUDGE_TURN_AFTER_TOOLS` now says the decider's instruction to decide by a tool no longer holds, and asks the judge
    to answer by calling `core_check_answer` once, with the object as its whole input. Reasoning in text first is
    allowed, and only the call is read.
- **`models/providers/anthropic/judge-call-answered.ts` (new).** Builds the next ask after an unread reply:
  1. the request that was sent, unchanged;
  2. the reply as the assistant turn (text and calls as they came, and thinking only with its signature);
  3. a user turn: one `is_error` `tool_result` per call, then the ask to call `core_check_answer`, with the judge
     loop's note and any steer.

  A decision tool's result says no decision tool runs for the check; the answer tool's says its answer was not read.
- **`models/providers/anthropic/messages.ts`.** An optional `replyArrived` hook hands back the reply's content
  blocks after the credential check and before reading.
- **`models/providers/anthropic/stream.ts`.** Keeps `thinking_delta` and `signature_delta` on thinking blocks. A
  turn that a `tool_result` answers must go back with its thinking unchanged, which Opus 5.5 always has.
- **`llm/anthropic/provider.ts`.**
  - A continuing judge's reply that arrived and then failed is held per attempt (`judges`).
  - On the next ask (`metadata.judgeAnswersInConversation`) the provider sends the answered turn
    (`answers_its_reply_in_the_conversation`). With nothing held, it continues the decider again with the note
    (`continues_the_decider_again`). After a JSON-text decider it still goes standalone (`standalone_after_unreadable`,
    the t979 cure for that transport).
  - `parseJudgeAnswer` reads only `core_check_answer`; a decision tool is `tool_call_not_answer`.
  - `parseToolCall` refuses a decider's `core_check_answer` as `tool_call_check_only`.
  - Every decision and every other judge call drops a held reply.
- **`models/failures/reply-account.ts` and `unreadable-reply.ts`.** Two new closed cases, `tool_call_not_answer` and
  `tool_call_check_only`, with their words for the model.
- **`result-verification/unreadable-asks/asks.ts` and `routing.ts`.**
  - A continuing judge's unreadable reply now sets `inConversation`, sticky for the check, and returns
    `in_conversation`. It never sets `standalone`.
  - The routing metadata is `continuesDecider` plus `judgeAnswersInConversation`.
  - `judgeStandaloneAfterUnreadable` is gone, and repeats are still steered (t1000).
- **`result-verification/flow-less/judge.ts`.** Comment and step-log note for the new step.
- **F7.** `llm/evidence-loop-decision.ts`: "leaving out looks" is now "leaving out steps that only observed". The
  two pinned prompt copies match: `llm/provider-task/tests/__snapshots__/explorer-system-prompt.txt` and
  `llm/deepseek/tests/system-prompt-pins.json`.
- **Tests.**
  - New: `result-verification/flow-less/tests/judge-tool-call-answered.test.ts`. It runs the real adapter, routing,
    harness and Flow-less judge over scripted replies shaped like the recording.
  - Updated: `llm/anthropic/tests/native-decision.test.ts` and `judge-continuation.test.ts`,
    `models/providers/anthropic/tests/judge-held-whole.test.ts` and `run-mwohvt8n-continuation.test.ts`,
    `result-verification/unreadable-asks/tests/asks.test.ts`, and `result-verification/flow-less/tests/judge-unreadable.test.ts`.

## Follow-up: real-sql OOM in `answer-first/completion/tests/judge-continues-decider.test.ts`

- **Cause (test).** The test's scripted wire answered every judge request with JSON text. Before t1065 the first
  unreadable continuation went standalone, where that text was the right format. Now each such reply is a prose-only
  `tool_call_missing`, answered in the conversation and asked again. Every call reported a fixed 100 input tokens however
  large the conversation grew, so the purse barely moved. The deadline is at least 120 s, and longer with the per-call
  timeout. The scripted replies are instant, so thousands of asks ran, and the test's own `bodies` array kept every
  growing request body: the heap ran out at about 136 s.
- **Product retention.** Per ask, the product keeps only the latest held turn, whose message objects are shared, not
  copied. The bodies are written to disk and released, and the step-log listing keeps a small row per step. So memory
  grows with the conversation, not faster than the calls.
- **Defect found and fixed (product).** The purse's hold before each call was priced on `measureInput`, which measured
  the judge's own standalone request, not the continuation or the in-conversation ask actually sent. So every
  continuation was held at a fraction of its size.
  - `measureInput` now measures exactly what `runTask` would send, through `continued(..., peek)`, which holds, drops and
    records nothing.
  - A continuation past the judge's own token limits now falls back to the judge's own request, as one past the window
    already did, so the harness never refuses it before it is sent.
- **Endings proven (new tests in `result-verification/flow-less/tests/judge-tool-call-answered.test.ts`).** A
  continuing judge that never answers readably, with no count of asks:
  - ends on the purse when calls are priced (not judged, `refusedByPurse`, fewer than 40 asks, every ask after the first
    in the conversation, nothing left pending on the purse);
  - ends at the check's deadline when calls cost next to nothing (not judged, "ran out of time").
- **Measure proven (new test in `llm/anthropic/tests/native-decision.test.ts`).** The continuation is measured larger
  than the judge's own request, and the in-conversation ask larger again. The ask's measure is within 40 tokens of
  bytes/3 of the body actually sent. Measuring twice gives the same number, and measuring held nothing back.
- **Script updated.** `answer-first/completion/tests/judge-continues-decider.test.ts`: the wire answers a judge whose
  request offers `core_check_answer` with a call of that tool, as a real model is now asked to.
