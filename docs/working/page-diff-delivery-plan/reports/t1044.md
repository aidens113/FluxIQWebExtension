## What happened (run-mwvgjb8c-5a1f2ae3)

The step files are in `C:/Users/osrs_/FluxStuff/lab-runs/2026-11-24/run-mwvgjb8c-5a1f2ae3/steps/`, not under
`test-runs/.../attempts/` as the brief said (the Lab writes `steps/` to its machine-wide `lab-runs` folder:
`packages/test-runner/src/lab-runs/lab-run-record.ts`).

- **Was pickup availability read? Yes.** Step `0022-tool-core.run_node` (`read_results`, page 1 of the
  filtered search) kept 15 rows. One of them was the right answer: a single roll at $1.28, rated 4.5, "Pickup today".
  This matches the oracle's expected item. Page 2 (`read_results_p2`, step 0040) kept 11 rows, the chosen $1.97 roll
  among them. Every row carried its pickup line (`pickup`, `..._all_lines`).
- **What the judge was shown** (`0042-judge/request.txt`). "1.28" appears 0 times.
  - `resultEvidence.looks`: page 2's 11 rows, whole. The run used page 2 as a look (a run without `add`).
  - `resultEvidence.working`: `[{"step":"read_results","position":9,"kept":15}]`, which is a count with no rows.
    The cause is `feed.ts:269`: a draft read that is not the answer goes to the judge as `{step, position, listKey, kept}`
    only, while a look goes with its rows (`feed.ts:270`).
  - The conversation prefix (the judge continued the decider, `meta.json` `continuation.used: true`). It held the
    `read_results` block with `firstRows` (3 rows) and `"restOfRows":"extracted holds all 15 kept rows."`, plus
    `{"core.view_reference":{"callId":"read_results","group":"holder:read"}}`. The judge request holds 29 view
    references and resolves none of them. Their whole values are in the decider's `core.current_view` tail, which
    comes after the last cache mark, and the continuation sends only blocks up to that mark.
  - `trail.entries`: `[]`, with `"shownAbove": "15 of the attempt's calls are in its evidence above..."`. That claim was
    false: `continuation.ts:86-89` counted every call that has a block above as held, even where the block held only
    references. The trail finding for a read carries counts only, by design (domain
    `judge-evidence/read.ts:8`: "Full cells ... reuse their existing source-page store"). The source-page store is
    `resultEvidence`, and for a working read it holds no rows. So the rows were in none of the judge's three channels.
- **Was the judge told the asked conditions? Yes.** `context.instructions` gives the request word for word: the
  store, pickup today, 4.5 or higher, the lowest price, one added for pickup.
- **Why it said yes.** It compared the rows it could see: page 2's 11 rows plus 3 of page 1's rows. Among those, the
  $1.97 roll is the cheapest that qualifies. Its instructions say "Nothing is wrong because it is not shown", "Answer no
  only on a concrete contradiction", and "unknown only where nothing in them bears on a part", so with no contradicting
  row in view it answered yes at 0.85 (`0042-judge/decision.json`).

**The decider made the wrong choice for the same reason** (outside this brief; reported only). In
`runtime/llm/decision-context/current-view.ts:28`, every read shares one held group, `holder:read`, and
`core.current_view` keeps only each group's latest whole value. Reading page 2 (0027 on) therefore evicted page 1's
rows from what the decider saw: "1.28" appears 4 times in 0023's request, 2 in 0025's, and 0 from 0027 on. Decision
0033 ("Page 2 results show the lowest-priced qualifying towel") chose among page 2 and page 1's 3 first rows. This is a
page-diff (W3) defect: two reads of one list must both stay visible to the model.

## What changed and why

- `!FluxIQ/packages/fluxiq/src/runtime/models/providers/anthropic/continuation.ts`: a call counts as held above only
  where every `core.view_reference` in its entry resolves to a group that a `core.current_view` entry inside the prefix
  holds. A reference that names no call or group is treated as unresolved. Calls that are not held keep their trail
  entries in the judge's message, and `shownAbove` counts only the calls that are held whole. The header records why.
  On its own, this does not put page 1's rows in front of the judge, because a read's trail finding carries counts
  only. It does stop the judge from being told that evidence it lacks is above it, and it gives the judge every
  page call's semantic finding again (states and read accounts).
- `.../anthropic/tests/judge-held-whole.test.ts` (new, provider-free): run-mwvgjb8c-5a1f2ae3's request shape with
  made-up values. A read whose rows appear above only by reference stays in the trail, a whole call is left out, a
  group the prefix itself holds resolves, and an unnamed reference does not resolve.
- `.../anthropic/tests/run-mwohvt8n-continuation.test.ts`: the third case now states the new rule against that run's
  recorded requests. The 6 calls whose views were references stay, the 3 whole ones are left out, and `shownAbove` says 3.

## Open questions or contradictions found

1. **Main fix, ready to apply in W3's area** (the supervisor's call, since W3 works in `answer-first/**`):
   - `answer-first/result-feed/feed.ts:269`: change
     `working: ledger.steps.filter(...).map((step) => ({ step, position, listKey, kept }))` to
     `working: ledger.steps.filter((step) => !group.includes(step)).flatMap((step) => step.pages.map((read) => page({ ...read, version: step.reads }, step.id, step.version, step.position)))`,
     the same page form that `pages` uses.
   - `runtime/result-verification/result-evidence/types.ts:61`: type `working` as
     `readonly (AutomationStudioResultEvidencePage | { step: string; position?: number; listKey?: string; kept: number })[]`,
     so stored bundles in the older form still read.
   - `answer-first/result-feed/read-evidence.ts:7`: `list(value.working, (entry) => page(entry) || working(entry))`,
     and decode `leftOut.found` for working pages as for pages and looks (line 15).
   - `result-evidence/from-batches.ts:29`: validate working entries that carry rows as pages.
   - `flow-less/window-fitting.ts:84`: fit `resultEvidence.working` beside `resultEvidence.looks`.
   - `flow-less/question.ts`: "Working reads and looks are evidence outside the result" stays true, and the trail note
     "Data rows, source pages ... are supplied separately in full" becomes true.
   - Test in `answer-first/result-feed/tests/`: a working read that kept a cheaper qualifying row than the answer
     appears with that row in `evidence().working`.
2. **Decider-side eviction** (`decision-context/current-view.ts`, `holder:read` shared by every read): this is why the
   model chose wrong. It is a page-diff (W3) defect.
