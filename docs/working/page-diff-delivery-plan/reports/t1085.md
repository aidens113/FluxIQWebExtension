## What changed and why

### 1. A repair judged yes replaces a kept Flow that did not stand (F3)

- `framework/automation-service/service/answer-first/task-memory/better-flow.ts`: the "both confirmed" row now takes
  `next` when the kept Flow just broke live (as before) **or** did not stand here: it is not `provenWhole`, this
  attempt ran it, and its whole run here was not judged yes. The header table and a "Proof beats no proof" paragraph
  say so, with run-mwz29d50's attempt 3 as the case.
- **Where the facts are.** The caller (`answer-first/attempt/remember.ts`) is in `answer-first/attempt/**`, which
  t1088 owns, so I left it alone and used the facts it already passes:
  - `wholeRunYes` (the attempt's run of the route's Flow was judged yes and went out);
  - `keptAssembles`, which `remember.ts` says only of the Flow the route ran (`undefined` otherwise, its docstring
    says so), so `keptAssembles === true` means "this attempt ran the kept Flow".
  A kept Flow another attempt saved meanwhile (`keptAssembles` undefined) keeps the old rule.
- **Test** (`task-memory/tests/better-flow.test.ts`, "proof beats no proof"): the run's two records as stored in its task
  memory (attempt ids, candidate ids, digests `02d89f44…` and `5b601b29…`, `provenWhole: false`, saved times, spend,
  shape; steps cut to position and action) with `how` as attempt 3 had it (`flowFailedLive: false, wholeRunYes:
  false, keptAssembles: true`). After: `latest`. Also: a proven-whole kept Flow, or one judged yes here, still stays;
  a kept Flow this attempt did not run still stays.
- One existing test encoded the old row ("leaves the label table to decide between two that assemble":
  kept unproven, `keptAssembles: true`, not judged yes, expected `k`). That is exactly the run's shape, so I changed it:
  a proven-whole kept Flow still wins, and the unproven one now gives way.

### 2. A keep or result is judged against the steps as the decision leaves them (Secondary)

- New `runtime/flow-authoring/draft/amendment/copy-after-drops.ts`: looks for the copy among the steps with the
  decision's drops applied (never the step being judged). Where the only copy is a step being dropped it says so
  (`leansOn`).
- `runtime/flow-authoring/draft/amendment/apply.ts`: collects the decision's `drop` steps (skipping struck ones), uses
  the helper for both `second_copy` checks (`result` and `keep`), and at the decision's end strikes a keep or result
  that leaned on a drop a guard put back (repeat hold, strand check), with `second_copy`. The t1069 retry loop then
  applies the decision again without that step's edits, so per-step atomicity holds.
- **Test** (`amendment/tests/copy-after-drops.test.ts`): 0058's shape "keep 18, result 18, drop 13" applies 3 and makes
  18 the kept result read; any order works; no drop, or a drop of another step, still refuses; and where the drop of
  13 is put back (13 is a repeat's `while`/`through`), 18 is refused `second_copy` and stays out with no `result`.

### 3. Which read is the result (K3)

**The cause is as the kestrel report infers, now confirmed from the recording.** Attempt 1 named step 6 the result:
0021 and 0023 both sent `[{step 6, repeat while 7}, {step 6, change: "result"}]`. The saved step kept `result: true`,
attempt 2 carried it as a ruled step (`isRuled`), and in `compose.ts` `markReads` a ruled kept step with `result` is
`named` and outranks every framework read, so 11, 12, 13 and 14 each came out `not_the_result` (0052's draft shows 11
`out: not_the_result` and Auction 9 `out: dead_end`). The feed then dropped each read from the ledger ("step 11 left
the draft … no read step is the result now"), while the carried step 6 was no ledger step at all (no page, no fields),
so the header said "the draft has no read step yet". Contrast: t1082's scripted attempt 1 never sent `result`, and its
attempt 2's fresh read 12 stayed in and became the result at once (0036, 0037). The `reads` code is stable across
attempts (bigbox's task memory: carried 13 and fresh 18 both `list:1b0926c191cf8d09`), so "the same list" can be
told across attempts. Bigbox run-mwz29d50 had the same shape (carried 13 with `result: true`, fresh 18), which is
why its model sent "keep 18, result 18, drop 13".

Changes:

- `runtime/flow-authoring/draft/compose/compose.ts` rule 2: a named pick that is ruled (carried) does not hold the result
  where a framework read of the same list (`reads`) is in the draft; the results are then the framework's own choice
  (all non-intermediate reads, in answer-first). The carried step itself is still copied as it is.
  - **Deviation from the brief, stated.** The brief says "a carried read *that has not run this attempt*". Compose
    cannot see whether a carried step ran this attempt: no step fact changes when the saved Flow's run or a forward pass
    runs it (the forward passes are recorded in `run.forwardPassed`, in `attempt/round.ts`, t1088's), and the only
    caller (`runtime/llm/evidence-loop.ts`) is outside my ownership. So the rule applies whether or not the carried read
    ran. Where it did run, both stay result reads and the ledger answers from the later one, which is rule 5's "the later
    read is the model's latest word". If the lead wants the narrower rule, `compose` needs one more predicate from the
    caller (for example `ranHere`), wired in `evidence-loop.ts` from `round.ts`.
- `framework/automation-service/service/answer-first/result-feed/feed.ts`, `isReadStep`: a kept read the model named the
  result (`result: true`) is a ledger read step, so with only the carried pick in the draft the header says "step 6 …
  v1 has read no page yet", the step the draft names, instead of "no read step yet". Also a step whose pages left the
  ledger with it.
- New `framework/automation-service/service/answer-first/result-feed/returned-pages.ts`, called from `feed.draft()`: a
  read step that comes back into the draft, reading the same way (`../result/version.ts`), gets back the pages it read
  before it left, from the left reads (`./left-reads.ts`). Not a page another step read since at the same address of
  the same list (t1074). So 0058's "keep 14, result 14" keeps page 1's 26 rows.
- `result-feed/index.ts` exports it.

Result-feed files K3 needed: `result-feed/feed.ts`, `result-feed/returned-pages.ts` (new), `result-feed/index.ts`,
`result-feed/tests/working-rows.test.ts`. Nothing in `result/` (the ledger) changed.

Proofs:

### The lead's question: are t1082's 9 rows the K3 credit defect?

**No.** I read `run-mwz3sdtg-ce7fe391` before the worktree was removed. Its attempt 2's 9 rows come from a different
cause:

- The replay stopped at the type step (0026). The scripted model then searched (9), pressed Auction (10), read page 1 with
  a new extraction (step 12, `extraction.2`, 26 rows), pressed Next page (13, `list: extraction.1`, which the draft
  left out `dead_end`) and ran `core.run_flow {from: 6}` (0039).
- That run read the carried step 6 (`extraction.1`) on page 2: "result 9 → 10 rows: step 6 read unplaced read 1 (20)".
  Next page then ended (0041), the run went on through 9, 10 and 12, and 12 read page 1 again: "result 10 → 9 rows: step
  12 read unplaced read 1 (26)".
- The answer is the ledger's output step only, the last read in the draft (`result/output-step.ts`): step 12's two
  readings of page 1. Step 6's page 2, which holds the tenth row, is a working read. The two steps did not fold (D2):
  they read with different extractions, so their fields differ.
- No rows were lost by a read being ruled the result after it read. Nothing in this run was `not_the_result` (attempt 1
  named no result), and my changes do not touch this path, so they will not make that attempt end with 10.
- **What it is.** The draft and the ledger disagree on what the result is when two non-intermediate reads of one list
  stand in the draft: compose (answer-first's `isResult`) counts both as result reads and keeps both in the Flow, while the
  ledger answers from the last one only. That is a design question for the lead (answer from every result read of the
  same list, merging pages by place, or have the draft keep one read per list); I did not change it.
