# Report: t1077, a label some cards carry is a mark, never glued into the title

Brief: fix, at the root and generically, the read-layer defect behind live kestrel run `run-mwyt3s48-b943f344`
(`reports/run-mwyt3s48-kestrel.md`, F1): list detection offered `link` (the title link's words, with the card's
"New listing" label glued on and no space) beside `link_text` (the clean title), and the model picked `link`.
Tree `fxwork/t1077-read-marker-words`, branch `task/t1077-read-marker-words`. Nothing committed.

## Outcome

Done. All three goals are built in the extension's extraction layer. Core and the domain are untouched.

1. A text element that says the same words on two items or more, but not on every item, is a word mark. It is
   offered as its own `badge` mark column.
2. A link whose words, once its word marks are left out, are one span's words offers only its URL. Links with words
   of their own keep their words column.
3. A text field reads its pieces the way the page draws them: a space between pieces drawn apart (blocks, and inline
   boxes spaced off by at least a space's width), nothing between pieces drawn flush. Where the page already had a
   space, values are unchanged.

On the real auction results page (`_nkw=kestrel+35&LH_Auction=1`), detection no longer offers the link's words.
Every card's title is one column. "New listing" is one `badge` column, and no offered column starts with the label.

## What changed and why

- `apps/extension/src/content/extraction/item-mark/word-mark.ts` (new), exported from `item-mark/index.ts`:
  - `wordMarks(run)` decides, once per selector over the whole run, whether the selector names a word mark.
  - The rule: missing from at least one item; the same words wherever present (the same rule as
    `constantBadgeName`, reused) on at least two items; not an amount (`valueShape`); at least two letters in a row.
  - The two-letters rule came from proof (b). The marketplace spells its ad label "Sponsored" one letter per span,
    with hidden noise letters between. Those single-letter spans were already offered as columns. Without the rule,
    nine of them became badge marks. A lone character is a letter, an initial or a size, not a label in words.
- `apps/extension/src/content/extraction/link-words/` (new: `own-words.ts`, `text-leaf.ts`, `index.ts`):
  - `offersOwnText` and `isTextLeaf` moved here from `infer-fields.ts`, with their history comments kept.
    `infer-fields.ts` was at 799 of its 800-line budget.
  - `offersOwnText` takes a `marked(leaf)` predicate. After the existing checks, it leaves out the marked spans. If the
    remaining words are empty, or equal one unmarked span's words, the link offers no words column.
  - The predicate is asked only when the link's words are not already one span's words.
- `apps/extension/src/content/extraction/infer-fields.ts`:
  - Builds `wordMarks(run)` once and passes it through `fieldSources` and `elementSources` to `offersOwnText`. A leaf
    is named by `selectorWithinItem`, as its own column would be named.
  - Marks a plain text column (not sensitive, not `lines`, not a definition, not a button) `mark: "badge"` when its
    selector is a word mark. Its label and key stay its path.
    - I did not relabel it to its words, as `badge-name.ts` does for icons. A page label such as "Price:" would then
      become key `price` on a column that only stands beside the price.
    - The model already sees the words as the column's sample, and the domain types a mark column as `flag`.
  - The header gained one paragraph. The file is now 777 lines.
- `apps/extension/src/content/sensitive-text.ts`:
  - `readableText` now adds a space between pieces the page draws apart. That covers blocks, as before, and also an
    inline box whose margin, border and padding on a side reach a quarter em of its own font (about a space's width).
  - Each side is decided on its own, so a margin on one side only splits that side. Right-to-left text swaps the sides.
  - Stand-ins that state no lengths read as before. Without a window the text is still `textContent`'s.
- `apps/extension/src/content/extraction/field-reader.ts`: `readText` reads with `textOutsideSensitiveControls(element,
  "readable")`. Every text field and every line of a `lines` field now reads as the page draws it. The comment says so.
- Tests:
  - `extraction/tests/word-marks.test.ts` (new, 9 tests) uses a synthetic news list with a styled "Breaking" label, not
    the auction page.
  - Counter-cases: a label on one story only; a link with words of its own; a label whose words vary; a label every
    story carries; a constant amount; a label spelled one letter per span.
  - Reader cases: spaced by margin, by padding, right-to-left, blocks; flush `<b>` and `<sup>` and a 2px hairline stay
    joined; pieces the page already spaced read exactly as before.
  - `extraction/tests/fake-shadow-dom.ts` gained an opt-in `layout: true`: computed styles from tag defaults plus the
    `style` attribute. Other tests are unaffected.
  - `apps/extension/e2e/content/tests/extraction/tests/structure-detection.spec.ts` gained proof (b). It went in this
    file because the spec directory is at its 25-file limit.

## Open questions or contradictions found

- The `lines` reader reads a text node between spans as a line, so the condition line reads
  `Pre-owned · · · Kestrel` (the page's own "·" becomes a line). That predates this change: the recorded run's rows
  carry the same value. Worth its own look.
- F2 (the data-layer re-placing of a glued column) is not built here. With F1 the glued column is not offered on this
  page, but a hand-written read could still name one.
- A word mark keeps its path label. If the lead wants it labelled by its words, as icon badges are, the risk is a
  page label like "Price:" becoming key `price`.
