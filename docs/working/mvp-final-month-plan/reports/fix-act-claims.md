## Audit (lead, 2026-10-06)

- The act judge `R/flow-bootstrap/instructed-acts/standing.ts` already holds one evidence rule (lane B, `cb783cba`, `step_only_opens_its_choices`). Everything else about a claim's step is advice only (`claim-doubt.ts`: "The claim is kept ... the whole-Flow test and its judge decide").
- What Core already holds about what a step did, without learning the domain's shape: the control's words (`words.target` / `control`), whether it answered a layer in front of the page (`interruption`, host-declared), whether the page moved before the next recorded step (`replay.from` compared whole, `automationStudioFlowDraftStepMovedTarget`), whether it flipped its control's chosen state (`toggle`), and its string values (`choice-evidence.ts`). The page change a press caused ("Cart (1)" was "Cart (0)") is computed by the domain (`domain/.../node-run/press-effect/page-changes.ts`) and shown to the model, but is not carried on the draft step.
- Decision: a claim of a lasting act (add_to, save, claim, submit, move) whose control words do not name the act, on a step whose own record shows it did something else, does not do the act. Three such records: it answered a layer (`step_only_clears_the_way`), it went to another page (`step_only_arrives`, the existing reason: "only opened a page"), or its control is one of the act's own options (`step_only_chooses`, `chooses` = that choice's id; C1b of mux74k5q). The checklist and the verdict name the step whose words name the act, when the draft has one (`instead`). A set or open act, a control with no words, and a control whose words name the act are judged as before. A toggle alone is not used: a heart or a clip toggle can be the save or the claim itself.
- Downgrade, not refusal at claim time: since t195 the checklist is information and completion is refused only by `permission.ts`; the downgrade reaches every reader (checklist, not-done lists, stall note, ending, the build-test judge). Refusing the claim when it is made needs the amendment and call handlers (t281 `apply.ts`, t287 `decision-handlers/amendment.ts`); described under "For other streams".
