## Proposed least, general fix and its test seams

**1. An unreachable group witness must not hold an act open (required, Core).**

- In `ledger.ts` `lookFirst`: when Core's own `lookAt(location)` lands and the read does not re-stamp a `look` group
  of that location, mark that group `unreachable` for the act.
- In `settlement-decision.ts`, treat `unreachable` like this:
  - **Other witness holds, and an undo landed or a change was seen:** the act settles `restored`. Every later trial's
    judge is told that the named list could not be read again, the same channel as `unchanged`.
  - **The unreachable group is the act's only witness:** the words say plainly that this act cannot be settled in
    this build (the `closed` wording in `settlement-words.ts`), so the brief ends honestly at once.
- Tests in `site-reset/tests/` beside the ledger and settlement-decision tests:
  - a toggle act with a named destination, whose look at its location reads a page without that group, settles
    after its toggle undo;
  - a group-only act gets the closed words;
  - a group read fresh at its location still behaves as today.

**2. Align the destination guidance (Core words).** In `create-baselines.ts`, qualify the body sentence the way the
`undoes-tool.ts` description already is: "where the press has no other witness; a control that shows its own state
needs none". Add a closing clause: "name a list read on a page that loads at its own address". The test sits beside
the words tests: the sentence carries the qualifier and names no realistic-scenario words.

**3. Say "already back" (Core).** In `undo-admission.ts`, when there is a toggle at the same place and
`declared.control.state === toggle.from`, answer with a distinct closed code (for example
`candidate.undo_already_back`) whose words are "its own control already shows the state the build found; nothing to
press", with the act's remaining `settle`. The test goes in `site-reset/tests/` beside the undo-admission tests.

Fix 1 also corrects contributor 4: act-9 would settle `restored`, so `standing()` would stop reporting it in place.

Not proposed:

- loosening the repeat guard (it was right at 0121, 0128 and 0153-0157);
- loosening the toggle admission (0151 must stay refused);
- anything that names this site, dialogs of this site, likes or posts in model guidance.

**(a) A builder repeats a failed or same-answer call until the guard ends its brief.** Seen in photo r2/r3/r4,

The guard is a correct symptom detector.

**One structural change:** an *actionable-answer contract*. Every Core refusal or `settle` that asks the model to act
carries a machine-checked next step. That is a tool and an input shape built only from handles and refs the model has
been shown, which Core has checked it can run in the current state. Where Core cannot form one, the gate does not ask:
it admits or settles with the leftover told to the judge, or it ends the brief with a closed
`gate_unsatisfiable:<code>` reason.

- Enforce it mechanically with a test over every refusal-words builder (site-reset `words.ts`,
  `settlement-words.ts`, candidate refusals): each must yield a next step or that closed ending.
- `refused-repeat.ts` should put the last non-repeat Core refusal code into the stall it returns, so a debug and the
  supervisor see which answer stranded the builder.

**(b) The supervisor ends right after a failed brief with budget left.** Seen in photo r4, social r2/r3, job r2/r3
and office r3 ($0.20 left). Photo r3 is the exception, where it reached brief 5. Guidance step 7 and
`completion.ts:134` allow ending after any one blocked or partial return.

**One structural change:** a *repair-first completion rule* in `project-agents/supervisor/completion.ts`. An `ended`
completion with purse left is refused (for example `project.completion_repair_first`) until every subflow that came
back blocked or partial has had at least one **changed** brief: a repair with `failingReceipt` or
`recordingExcerpt`, or a `project.amend_plan`. The rule does not apply when the return carries a Core-closed terminal
reason: the person declined a permission, a person-only check, the purse is spent, or (a)'s `gate_unsatisfiable`.
Step 7's wording changes to match.

- The test goes in `project-agents/supervisor/tests/` beside the completion tests: after one blocked return with money
  left, `ended` is refused; after a changed brief also failed, or with a closed terminal reason, it is admitted.
- In this run, (a)'s closed reason would correctly have let it end. With fix 1, there would have been no such reason
  and a repair would have run.
