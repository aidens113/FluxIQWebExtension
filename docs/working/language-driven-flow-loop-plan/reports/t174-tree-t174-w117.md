# t174-w117: the chat stops following the stream (D16); stale pace docs

## Outcome

Done. I found the cause of D16 and reproduced it in real Chromium with nobody scrolling. I fixed it in `scroll-follower.ts`, with failing-first unit tests. Both stale pace passages are corrected.

## What changed and why

### Cause (D16)

`createScrollFollower`'s scroll handler (`scroll-follower.ts:57` before the fix) ended following on any scroll event that moved `scrollTop` and left the view more than 32 px off the bottom. It treated that as the person scrolling up. The browser makes such scrolls on its own too.

The case that bites is Chrome's scroll anchoring. Suppose a card above the top of the view gets shorter while the newest content below gets taller by the same amount. The content's total height does not change, so the ResizeObserver on the column never fires. Anchoring moves `scrollTop` up to keep the top line in place, and the scroll event that follows reports a view that "moved" and is "off the bottom". Following then ended for good: the chat stayed parked and "↓" showed.

That matches screenshots 00015 to 00020 and UI-review pictures 11 to 15. There the view sits about one live-line height above the bottom, and from then on the hand-off question and the build's ending are never on screen.

Evidence (scratch probe `.../scratchpad/w117/probe.mjs`): the real `scroll-follower.ts` bundled into headless Chromium 1161, with layout like `chat.css`. It ran 400 random content changes, contentChanged after 60% of them, and no person input at all. Before the fix:

- Seed 1 failed at step 287: `"287: swap heights top=14278 max=14346 -> ... following=false"`, coming from `top=14346 max=14346` the step before.
- Seed 5 failed at step 33: `"33: swap heights top=1766 max=1851 -> ... following=false"`.

In both cases the height was unchanged and anchoring moved the view up by 68 and 85 px. With contentChanged after every change, the same probe never failed (10 seeds × 400 steps). So the trigger is a layout change that keeps the height and is not followed by `renderAll`. The live run showed the outcome, but I could not tell from it which panel change was the trigger. Candidates are the composer's rAF fit, anything else that reflows outside `renderAll`, and the Lab's own panel interaction.

### Fix: `apps/extension/src/panel/chat/view/scroll-follower.ts`

Only the person ends following now, the way ChatGPT behaves. A scroll that leaves the bottom ends following only while the person is scrolling, which means one of these:

- a `wheel` or `touchmove` in the last `PERSON_SCROLL_MS` (1 s);
- a scroll key (arrows, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End, Space) pressed outside a text field;
- the scroll bar held down: a `pointerdown` whose target is the scrolled element itself, until `pointerup` or `pointercancel`.

A press on a card's button does not count. Any other scroll that leaves the bottom is the browser's, and a follower goes back down. Coming back to the bottom still resumes following, and "Jump to latest" and `followNow` are unchanged.

`ScrollHost.addEventListener` now takes any event type, and there is an optional `ownerDocument` for key presses and drag releases outside the view. `HTMLElement` fits both, so `chat-panel.ts` needed no change.

### Tests: `apps/extension/src/panel/chat/view/tests/scroll-follow.test.ts`

- The fake host separates `scroll` (the person: a wheel turn, then the scroll) from `shift` (the browser moving the view on its own). The two tests that modelled the follower's own late scroll event now use `shift`.
- New: "the browser moving the view up on its own never ends following: only the person scrolling does". It replays the measured 68 px anchoring shift, then checks that the person's own scroll up still ends following.
- New: "dragging the scroll bar or a scroll key counts as the person; a click inside a card does not".

## Open questions or contradictions found

- **"A question for the person and the final answer are always brought into view."** After this fix they are, unless the person scrolled up themselves. If the person did scroll up, the only cue is the bare "↓" jump button. A clearer "New messages" or "FluxIQ needs you" control, or pulling a hand-off into view, would need `panel/chat/chat-panel.ts` (which owns the jump button and knows `answerIn`) and `chat.css`. Both are outside this brief, so I did not do it.
- **Person window.** Within 1 s of the person's input, a browser-made scroll can still end following. That is a deliberate trade-off.
- **Lab interaction.** The Lab's person simulation uses `scrollIntoViewIfNeeded` on panel targets (`person-simulation/tab.ts:60`). That is not person input, so it no longer ends following, and the follower brings the view back down after it.
