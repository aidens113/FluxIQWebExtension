## What changed and why

### 1. Cause 2: the choice-order sentence no longer moves a choice ahead of its page
- `flow-bootstrap/instructed-acts/choice-order.ts`: `automationStudioInstructedChoiceAfterAct` now takes the two Step
  objects, `steps` and the optional `act` (it used to take two positions). It picks one of three sentences:
  - **The claim looks wrong** (`./claim-doubt.ts` holds: the act's step led to another page, and its control names
    neither the act's verb nor its kind). The sentence says the claim of the act on step M looks wrong, says not to move
    step N before step M, and says to name the act on the step that does it, after step N. There is no `reorder`.
  - **Either-or, no reorder.** This fires when the act's step led to another page, or the choice's step acted on a page
    the act's step had not reached (`replay.from` differs, compared whole). The sentence says do not reorder. It then
    says that either the claim on step M looks wrong, or step N changes what step M already did (a line in a cart).
  - **Otherwise**, the old sentence, including `amend_draft reorder on step N with to M`.
  - It is still information, never a refusal.
- `checklist.ts` (`madeAfterAct`) and `check.ts` (`choicesAfterAct`) pass the steps, the draft and the act.

### 2. Cause 3: an act claimed on a step that only led elsewhere is answered with one sentence, and the claim is kept
- `flow-draft/act-claim.ts`: new `automationStudioFlowDraftClaimedStepLeftPage(steps, step)`.
  - It finds the next proposable step after this one by position, decided no earlier and carrying `replay.from`.
  - It then asks whether that step found the target elsewhere, using `automationStudioFlowDraftStepMovedTarget` from
    `verify-only.ts`.
  - The header records the binding rule: claims are information, never refusals.
  - `automationStudioFlowDraftClaimAct` is unchanged.
- New `flow-bootstrap/instructed-acts/claim-doubt.ts`: `automationStudioInstructedActClaimDoubt(act, step, steps)`
  returns one sentence when:
  - the step left the page, and
  - the act is not an `open` act, and
  - the control words (`words.target ?? control`) name neither the act's verb nor its kind words.

  So an Add to cart that redirects to the cart, or a Place order, is never doubted.
- New `flow-bootstrap/instructed-acts/kind-words.ts`: `KIND_WORDS` moved out of `check.ts` unchanged and exported as
  `AUTOMATION_STUDIO_INSTRUCTED_ACT_KIND_WORDS`. `check.ts` imports it. `claim-doubt.ts` and `kind-words.ts` are not in
  the barrel; they are internal readings.
- `checklist.ts`: an act that is `done` carries `claimSaid` when there is a doubt, and the act stays done. It reaches
  the model every decision through `automationStudioInstructedActsChecklistValue`, which spreads item fields, so no
  other file needed editing. It reaches the judge through `buildTest.checklist`.
- I chose the checklist rather than the amendment answer. A claim made as a step runs, like 0011's listing click, has
  no next step yet, so nothing can be known at claim time. The checklist on the next decision is the first point where
  the page change is visible. The amendment answer lives in `flow-draft/amendment.ts` and `llm/evidence-loop.ts`, which
  I do not own.
