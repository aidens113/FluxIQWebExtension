## What P4 now does, per plan item

1. **One "back at its place" answer, keyed by one place per act.**
   - `F/reset-settlement/own-control.ts` `automationStudioSiteResetActOwnControl` picks one place per act: its retained
     original's, else its toggle's, else its press's, else its spot's.
   - The undo admission (`F/reset-undo/admission.ts`, toggle and value paths) reads that place, and so does the
     settlement (`judge.ts`, `control-tracker.ts`).
   - The settlement asks for a press back only when a fresh read shows the state the act set. That is the one state
     the admission admits as `own`.
   - Any other state, or a control no longer drawn, closes the act as `control_other` (`no_admissible_undo`). Before,
     that case was left open as `toggle`, which was the mvrj-a44a livelock shape.
2. **No judgement on a stale in-place read.** A list read in place after any landed undo in the ledger is never
   judged until a fresh read, not only after undos of the acts that list witnesses (`judge.landedUndos`,
   `observations.ts`). This is the mvrj-b6dc shape.
3. **One answer, never two.** `F/reset-answers/agreeing.ts` now follows the ledger's verdict.
   - A refusal whose own act is listed apart says `no_call` and never lists that act to undo.
   - This reverses t772 R2, which moved the act back to undo while the next answer listed it apart again.
4. **Every act to undo has an admissible call, or is closed.**
   - Each act in `actsToUndo` has an admissible call named by its settle words. Otherwise it moves to
     `actsUnsettleable` with a closed `gate_unsatisfiable`, naming what it did, the call and what it failed.
   - A property sweep over 220 ledger states proves it (`F/site-reset/tests/ledger-act-to-call.test.ts`).
   - The reloading `modify_existing` press (value witness) already held after t772 and is covered as a guard.
5. **`undoes` is an optional hint; Core pairs an undo by inverse effect (CC4).**
   - `F/reset-undo/claims.ts` `pairOf`/`paired` handles a landed press that names no act. When it reverses exactly one
     earlier open act on the same resource, it is recorded as that act's undo.
   - "Reverses" means the act's effect places come back exactly, or the press's toggle at the same place has from and
     to swapped. Two matching acts pair nothing, `irreversible` never pairs, and a hinted `undoes` keeps today's path.
   - The CC4 type `AutomationStudioSiteResetEffect` is in `F/site-reset/contracts.ts` and is parsed tolerantly in
     `act-places.ts` `effectOf`.
   - The binding's `startState` answer type (`runtime/llm/harness-options/binding.ts`) gained an optional `effect`, and
     `F/site-reset/start-check.ts` passes it through. Without that pass-through, which I added at integration, a client
     record never reached the ledger.
   - The attach list applies only on the act's own page. The real gap was another page in the same tab, because a web
     resource is the whole tab.
6. **`cannotUndo` defaulted** (`F/trial-defaults/before-trial.ts`, shared by both trial gates).
   - When omitted, it defaults to the acts Core itself listed apart as unsettleable. A supplied list narrows to acts
     Core listed.
   - I narrowed the worker's version at integration. It defaulted to every listed act, which would tell the judge that
     acts the site can still undo cannot be undone (untruthful). It also broke 5 project-build tests
     (`tests/site-reset-build`, `site-reset-irreversible`), which all pass now.
7. **words.ts P4 items** were already true: `outsideBrief` points to `project.amend_plan`; a call under `undoes` is
   never told it is outside the brief; `retryRefused` no longer exists.
8. **Ledger split, made first, with no behaviour change.**
   - Before the split, site-reset/ had 25 files (the hard limit), its tests/ had 25, ledger.ts had 800 lines and the
     class had 39 methods.
   - A subdirectory of site-reset breaks the 9-segment depth rule, which never ratchets. So the split follows the t743
     pattern instead: siblings `F/reset-undo/` and `F/reset-ledger/` (answers.ts, recording.ts, claims.ts, act.ts,
     leftover.ts, trial-stamp.ts, ...).
   - The facade stays at `F/site-reset/ledger.ts` (470 lines, 27 methods). Exported names are unchanged.
   - The test count was 54 files / 689 tests both before and after the split.
9. **W4: one place token per act (web domain).** The t772 per-page token stands, and no source change was needed.
