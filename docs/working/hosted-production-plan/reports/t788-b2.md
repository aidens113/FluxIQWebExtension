## Contract defects and gaps (lead action needed)

1. **`ops:Snapshot` collides; the frozen catalog schema cannot express it.** Seven operator programs
   (background-tasks, compute-control, database-manager, deployment-sync, docs, production-runner,
   runtime) each register `snapshot`. Under the contract's rule `action === iamActionName(service,
   endpoint)` all seven are `ops:Snapshot`. But `iamActionCatalogSchema` refuses duplicate actions,
   and an entry holds one `source`. Interim: the generator merges them into one entry (source = the
   lowest programId) and lists all seven in `sharedActions`. They agree on access level, permission
   and classification, so parity is unaffected.
   **Proposed exact change** in `packages/contracts/src/iam/action/catalog-entry.ts`:
   - Replace `source: iamActionSourceSchema` with
     `sources: z.array(iamActionSourceSchema).min(1).max(16)`.
   - The superRefine then checks that every `endpoint` source yields `entry.action`.
   - The `ops` resource FRN `program/<programId>` already tells the programs apart.
2. **The generated catalog file is not written.** The plan puts it at
   `packages/contracts/src/iam/actions.generated.ts`, which is under the frozen contracts.
   `renderIamActionCatalogModule(catalog)` renders its source. A lane that may edit contracts
   writes the file and adds the drift check (one line in the catalog's live test).
3. **The 15 app routes are not in the catalog or the parity test.** They live in `apps/web` (lane
   B3), and the plan names no action for them.
4. Open from t784, decided provisionally here:
   - The role-session FRN is the role FRN.
   - The 7 operator programs map to `ops`: database-manager, background-tasks, compute-control,
