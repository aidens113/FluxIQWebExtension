## What changed and why

Paths are relative to `apps/extension/src/`.

**D7: "Waiting for you" stayed up after Continue, and the answer was echoed three times.**
- `background/activity/unit-situation.ts`: Core's closing `ask` row (`detail.kind "ask"`, status `succeeded` or `failed`) now clears the robot-check flag. Before this, the flag cleared only when a later page action succeeded or a run moved to its next step. So the headline could stay on "Waiting for you: finish the check on the page" after the person pressed Continue. The new pacer test reproduced exactly that: `outcome` stayed `waiting`.
- `background/activity/pacer.ts`: the closing ask row with the person's own resolution (`answered`, `allowed` or `declined`) shows at once and puts no detail on the status. So "You pressed Continue." is no longer repeated as the status line.
- `content/activity-overlay/status-dwell.ts`: when the waiting state starts or ends (the `attention` mark appears or goes), the overlay redraws at once instead of waiting out the 1.6 s dwell. In the run, the Lab answered within the dwell of the ask's words, so the page kept showing "Waiting for you" while the panel had already moved on (00012 was taken about 100 ms after the answer).
- `panel/chat/conversation/ask-copy.ts`: an answered robot-check ask now gets a new presentation state, `{ state: "silent" }`. The check is recognised by Core's option ids `person_done` and `person_stop`, not by its words. The card "Robot check · Done. You pressed Continue." is now the one place the answer is told. `ask-controls.ts` (not mine) has no case for `silent`, so it renders an empty `turn-ask` div. An expired ask still says "FluxIQ stopped waiting for an answer."

**D10: the status followed a finished step instead of the newest phase.**
- `pacer.ts` `opensStage`: a stage Core announces now skips the 1.2 s pace. That covers a `note` row with no `ref` ("Judging the Flow"), a settled `check` row titled "Result check", and the person's answer. Stages come a few times per unit of work, so they cannot cause flicker. Completion-check rows are still paced; the t174 replay fixture would otherwise give three detail changes in one second. At step 0068, "— done" arrived about 10 ms before "Judging the Flow", and the pacer used to hold "Judging" for 1.2 s.
- `unit-situation.ts`: a passed result check (`check`, status `succeeded`, title "Result check") now ends the repair. Without this, "Fixing your Flow" stayed up through the cross-check after the judge passed (00015). Core sends no activity row during the cross-check (`action-permissions/cross-check.ts` emits none). So "The result answers the request" remains the last detail, as the latest thing Core said, under the "Building your Flow" headline.

**Moment 7: the overlay disappeared for about 200 ms.** This was a navigation, not a phase switch. The gap sample (04:39:00.681 + 1.2 s = 04:39:01.88) falls just after step 0052 started navigating the same tab to `/cart` (04:39:01.780). The overlay came back at a different position (y 327 → 638), because a new document places it from scratch. Nothing in my files unmounts the overlay on a phase switch; a new relay test guards that.
- `activity-relay.ts` `noteContentReady`: a tab the overlay is already drawn in is now answered without looking up the tab list again, which saves one `chrome.tabs.get` round trip while the new page has no overlay.

**Moment 8 and 00013: the overlay was not on the tab in front.** The overlay was drawn only in the single tab `OverlayTarget.resolve` returned. That is the driven tab, which is the tab of the last runtime command. In 00013 the Flow's test was working while a newly opened item tab was in front, and that tab had no overlay.
- `overlay-target.ts`: new `resolveAll()` returns the driven tab, plus the focused window's front tab when that is a different page the overlay may draw on. Extension pages, browser pages and FluxIQ's own origins are still refused.
- `activity-relay.ts`: the relay can now draw in several tabs at once. There is a new optional dep, `overlayTabIds`. The relay sends to all target tabs in parallel, takes the overlay down only in tabs that left the set, and keeps `drawnIn` as a set. Without the new dep it behaves as before, using `automationTabId` alone.
- The sample itself was partly a Lab artifact. `packages/test-runner/src/run-scenario/ui-review/choose-scenario-tab.ts` decides which tab is in front by URL. Two tabs were at the same `/search?...` URL, so the run's own background tab was sampled. Its picture `08-mid-build-scenario.png` has a cookie banner and a 1280x720 viewport, unlike the tab visible in 00012, which did show the overlay.

**00004: the pill sat at the right edge, behind the side panel.**
- `content/activity-overlay/placement/choose-placement.ts`, new rule 0: placement now chooses among the left corners and the left midpoint only. It searches the right side as well only when every left place, full pill and narrow, lies wholly on fixed parts of the page. In 00004 a sticky part on the left sent the pill to the right midpoint, which the Lab's emulated 1280-pixel viewport puts under the side panel. Two existing expectations changed to match: "top-right" became "top-left", and "right" became "left".

## Open questions or contradictions found

1. `background/connection.ts` (not mine) needs one line in the `ActivityRelay` deps to turn on moment 8 / 00013: `overlayTabIds: () => overlayTarget.resolveAll()`. Until then the relay falls back to `automationTabId`.
2. `connection.ts` `deliverToTab` calls `ensureContentScript`, which sends `fluxiq.ping` before each send. For a document that has just announced itself this round trip is not needed. Skipping it, for example with a `noteContentReady` path that calls `sendToTab` directly, would shorten the navigation gap further.
3. The Lab's `choose-scenario-tab.ts` decides which tab is in front by URL. With several tabs at the same URL it samples a background tab, so moment 8's "absent ×16" partly measured the wrong tab.
4. The `silent` state drops the turn note for an answered robot check. If the worker restarts and its activity history is lost, the check's card is gone, and nothing in the thread says Continue was pressed.
5. With multi-tab drawing, an unrelated page in front of the person also shows the status while FluxIQ works. That follows the standard ("visible whenever FluxIQ works"); the supervisor should confirm it is wanted.
6. Placement rule 0 can cover part of a fixed element on the left even when a clear right corner exists. That is deliberate (the Lab panel hides the right side), but in a real browser the side panel narrows the viewport instead of covering it.
